Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

GT Freight Lines LLC ("GT Freight Lines," "we," "us," or "our") respects the privacy of customers, freight partners, drivers, vendors and website visitors. This policy explains what information we collect, why we use it and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

We may collect information you provide when you call, email, text or work with us, including:

  • Name, company, job title and contact details such as phone number and email address.
  • Freight and service details, including origin, destination, commodity, dimensions, weight, dates, reference numbers and operational instructions.
  • Messages, call details, documents and other communications related to a shipment or business relationship.
  • Billing, payment and compliance information needed to provide transportation services or meet legal requirements.

This website does not include an advertising tracker. Our hosting and communications providers may process limited technical information, such as IP address, browser type and access logs, to deliver and protect their services.

How we use information

  • Respond to inquiries and provide quotes or service information.
  • Plan, perform and document transportation services.
  • Send conversational and informational messages about pickups, deliveries, load status, documents and service coordination.
  • Maintain business records, prevent misuse and comply with legal, insurance, safety and regulatory obligations.

How information is shared

We do not sell personal information. We may provide relevant information to customers, brokers, shippers, consignees, drivers, insurers, regulators and service providers when needed to arrange or perform transportation, protect our operations or comply with law.

No mobile opt-in or text message consent will be shared with third parties or affiliates.

Retention and security

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for operations, recordkeeping, dispute resolution and legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete information, subject to legal and operational recordkeeping requirements. To stop text messages, reply STOP. For text-message help, reply HELP or contact us directly.
